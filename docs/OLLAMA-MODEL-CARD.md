## The Holy Trinity: Data + AI + Automation.

- **Data:** bring your offer, customer context, approved proof and real conversations.
- **AI:** work with MangoMagic to research, draft, question and improve.
- **Automation:** test the work, then make the useful parts repeatable with the tools in your app.

## One command. Your whole setup.

Paste into Terminal on your Mac:

```bash
bash -c 'f=$(mktemp) || exit; trap "rm -f \"$f\"" EXIT; curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mango-magic/mangomagic/main/setup-operations.sh -o "$f" && bash "$f" --with-mangomagic'
```

Creates your **AI Operations** workspace, adds the **Build My Assistant** playbook and six specialist role templates, installs MangoMagic through Ollama and registers it with ChatGPT. It gracefully quits and reopens ChatGPT.

Then connect the folder to your project and use **Build My Assistant** to personalise the way it works with you. Account sign-in may be required. Folders and templates do not automatically launch workers or create schedules.

## Choose your Mango.

| Choice | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Light** | Everyday drafting and straightforward tasks. |
| **Mango** | More involved outreach, objections and sales decisions. |
| **Super Mango** | Your hardest questions and deeper analysis. |

These select lighter, high and maximum reasoning effort on the same model. ChatGPT's native labels remain **Low / Light, High and Max**. More thinking can use more time and tokens; it does not guarantee a better answer or a fixed speed difference.

**Bring images.** Use screenshots, charts and other images alongside your question. Tool use depends on what your app provides and authorises.

## Built for real buyer conversations.

Direct Australian English. A buyer's actual problem. Proof beside claims. Objections addressed directly. One useful next move. No invented customer stories, metrics or testimonials.

Try these:

- “Use this conversation to draft the next follow-up. Keep it specific and give the buyer one useful next step.”
- “Review this screenshot. What is the buyer actually asking for, and what should I say next?”
- “Challenge this outreach before I send it. Check relevance, evidence, tone and the ask.”

## Access and usage.

For the Mac setup, you need the supported ChatGPT desktop app, internet access and an Ollama account with cloud access. [Ollama usage](https://ollama.com/pricing) is separate from your ChatGPT subscription. The installer does not purchase a plan or credits.

MangoMagic uses the business context you actually supply. Adding documents personalises its working context and instructions; it does not retrain model weights. [Technical release notes](https://github.com/mango-magic/mangomagic/blob/main/docs/TECHNICAL-NOTES.md).
